Kestrel Deep

The freighter Kestrel has broken its back on a moon. Salvage tech Mara Vesh has forty-five minutes to work through the tunnels of thirteen buckled decks before the reactor breaches, and the security troopers are still on duty.

Reactor breach in 45:00

Find each deck's keycard, get past the troopers, and reach the exit before the core goes.
